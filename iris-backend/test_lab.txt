
    Titration Lab Experiment
    Objective: Determine the concentration of an unknown acid solution.
    
    Procedure:
    1. Gather all equipment including burette, conical flask, and pipette
    2. Rinse the burette with distilled water then with the NaOH solution
    3. Fill the burette with 0.1M NaOH solution to the zero mark
    4. Pipette exactly 25ml of the unknown acid into a conical flask
    5. Add 3 drops of phenolphthalein indicator to the flask
    6. Slowly add NaOH from the burette while swirling the flask
    7. Stop when the solution turns permanently pink
    8. Record the volume of NaOH used
    9. Repeat for concordant results
